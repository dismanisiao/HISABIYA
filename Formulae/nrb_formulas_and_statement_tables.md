# NRB Financial Statements: Formulas and Statement Tables

Columns for every statement: **Group** (Current Year, Previous Year) and **Bank** (Current Year, Previous Year).
Formulas below follow the row order of the NRB format. Items marked *(standard)* are standard definitions, not written in the NRB document.

## 5. Statement of Cash Flows

| Line Item                                                               | Formula                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Operating cash flows before changes in operating assets and liabilities | Interest received + Fees and other income + Dividend received + Other operating receipts − Interest paid − Commission and fees paid − Payments to employees − Other expenses paid |
| (Increase)/Decrease in operating assets                                 | Change in due from NRB, placements, trading assets, loans to B/FIs, loans to customers, other assets (increase is negative)                                                       |

| **Net cash flow from operating activities**                             | Above − Income taxes paid                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Net cash used in investing activities**                               | Sale receipts of securities, property, intangibles, investment property + Interest and dividends received − Purchases of the same                                                 |

## 7. Note Calculations (roll-forwards and totals)

| Note                                 | Calculation                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 4.6.1 Impairment (B/FIs)             | Closing = Opening + Charge for the year − Recoveries/reversal − Amount written off                                                                                  |
| 4.7 Loans measured at amortised cost | Gross loans − Collective impairment − Individual impairment                                                                                                         |
| 4.7.1 Loans by product               | Sum of products + Interest receivable = Grand total                                                                                                                 |
| 4.7.2 / 4.7.3                        | By currency and by collateral. Totals must equal the 4.7.1 grand total                                                                                              |
| 4.7.4 Specific allowance             | Closing = Opening + Charge − Recoveries − Write-offs ± Exchange variance ± Other movement                                                                           |
| 4.7.4 Collective allowance           | Closing = Opening + Charge/(reversal) ± Exchange variance ± Other movement                                                                                          |
| 4.7.4 Total allowances               | Specific allowances + Collective allowances                                                                                                                         |
| 4.13 / 4.14 Net book value           | Cost − Accumulated depreciation/amortisation and impairment                                                                                                         |
| 4.13 / 4.14 Cost roll-forward        | Opening + Acquisition + Capitalisation − Disposal ± Revaluation/adjustment                                                                                          |
| 4.15 Deferred tax                    | Net deferred tax = Deferred tax assets − Deferred tax liabilities. Closing = Opening ± Origination/reversal                                                         |
| 4.20 / 4.20.1 Deposits               | Institutional + Individual = Total. Currency-wise total must equal the same figure                                                                                  |
| 4.22 Provisions                      | Closing = Opening + Provisions made − Provisions used − Provisions reversed + Unwind of discount                                                                    |
| 4.22.1 Defined benefit               | Net obligation = Present value of obligations − Fair value of plan assets                                                                                           |
| 4.22.3 Obligation movement           | Closing = Opening + Actuarial losses + Current service cost and interest − Benefits paid                                                                            |
| 4.22.4 Plan assets movement          | Closing = Opening + Contributions + Expected return ± Actuarial gain/(loss) − Benefits paid                                                                         |
| 4.26.2 Share ownership               | Each class % = Class amount ÷ Total paid up capital × 100                                                                                                           |
| 4.28 Contingents                     | Total = Acceptances + Bills for collection + Forward contracts + Guarantees + Underwriting + Other                                                                  |
| 4.28.4 Leases                        | Sub total = Not later than 1 year + 1 to 5 years + Later than 5 years                                                                                               |
| 4.41 Income tax                      | Total tax = Current tax (current year + prior adjustments) + Deferred tax                                                                                           |
| 4.41.1 Tax reconciliation            | Total tax = Profit before tax × Tax rate + Non-deductible expenses effect − Exempt income effect ± Other. Effective tax rate = Total income tax ÷ Profit before tax |

## 8. Quarterly and Audit Comparison Formulas

| Item                                     | Formula                                               |
| ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Variance in amount                       | Audited − Unaudited                                   |
| Variance in %                            | (Audited − Unaudited) ÷ Unaudited × 100               |
| Free profit after regulatory adjustments | Net profit ± Regulatory adjustments per NRB Directive |

### Quarterly ratios *(standard definitions, not written in the NRB document)*

| Ratio                      | Definition                                                         |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Capital fund to RWA        | Capital fund ÷ Risk weighted assets × 100                          |
| NPL to total loan          | Non performing loans ÷ Total loans × 100                           |
| Loan loss provision to NPL | Total loan loss provision ÷ Total NPL × 100                        |
| Cost of funds              | Interest expense on funding ÷ Average interest-bearing liabilities |
| Credit to deposit ratio    | Total loans ÷ Total deposits × 100 (computed as per NRB Directive) |
